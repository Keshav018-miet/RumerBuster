Critical Challenge

1. The Problem Statement

The digital information ecosystem is facing an unprecedented crisis. With the democratization of generative artificial intelligence and high-speed social networks, fabricated stories and altered media spread six times faster than verified facts.

📢 Viral Disinformation Crisis

Unverified rumors, out-of-context headlines, and coordinated propaganda campaigns can destabilize public health, disrupt financial markets, and polarize democratic elections before manual fact-checkers can respond.

🎭 Deepfakes & Synthetic Media

Advanced neural models (GANs, diffusion transformers) enable bad actors to clone voices, generate synthetic facial expressions, and swap identities in video with high realism, eroding trust in audiovisual evidence.

⏱️ The Verification Bottleneck

Traditional verification is slow, labor-intensive, and fragmented. Journalists require hours or days to trace citations, analyze forensic metadata, and reach consensus, leaving the public unprotected during breaking events.

End-to-End Pipeline

3. Main Functionality & Core Modules

RumorBuster provides five specialized forensic modules linked through a central chain of custody:

📰

1. News Analysis & NLP Classifier

Ingests text statements, headlines, or articles. Uses scikit-learn TF-IDF n-gram vectorization and an online Passive-Aggressive Classifier to instantly evaluate heuristic linguistic markers of deception.

Sub-millisecond inference (2.4 ms) • Confidence Scoring
🌐

2. Live Web Fact-Checker (RAG)

Extracts entities (spaCy NER) and queries multi-source news indexes (Google News RSS, Inshorts, NewsAPI). Passes raw verified articles as context into Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for consensus reasoning without hallucination.

Multi-source cross-reference • Full citation links
🧭

3. Origin Tracer (Ground Zero)

Uncovers where a rumor or webpage first appeared online. Queries Internet Archive (Wayback Machine CDX API) for the earliest snapshot timestamp, ICANN RDAP for domain registration age, and logs submitter node geolocation.

Legal public APIs • Burner domain risk scoring • Recurrence
🖼️

4. Image & Face Forensics

Executes MTCNN face landmark detection with a 30% bounding margin. Passes face crops to an EfficientNet-B7 deepfake classifier (DFDC winner architecture) while extracting embedded camera EXIF signatures.

PyTorch tensors • Blending artifact detection • EXIF dumps
🔒

5. Cryptographic File Integrity

Generates dual-layer hashes: SHA-256 for bit-for-bit tampering detection, and perceptual hash (pHash) based on Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) for media that underwent compression or format conversion.

Immutable Chain of Custody • SQLite audit history
🗄️

6. Verifiable Forensic Case Reports

Assigns a unique UUID to every analysis case. Logs full technical metadata, confidence ratings, timestamps, and supporting sources into an audit ledger exportable as printable legal defense documents.

Court-admissible audit log • 1-click PDF print export
