How RumorBuster Finds & Verifies Rumors

RumorBuster combats misinformation through intelligent multi-source analysis. When a claim or headline is submitted, the engine extracts key entities, cross-references real-time verified news networks and live web indexes, and leverages generative AI to analyze context, determine factual consensus, and provide transparent truth ratings with credible sources.
